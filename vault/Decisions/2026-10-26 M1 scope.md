## For future Claude
M1 scope cut as the producer proposed; one CEO item (desktop executable) is split out.

**IN:** one room from server config, two player slots (open to claimed only, no password), placeholder player id, one flat map, one declarative unit placed by the map with an owner slot, one order (move), configurable tick, command validation, replication, interpolation, a lockfile with one entry checked by hash.
**OUT, with owner:** index (M8), rooms and passwords (M9), real identity and reconnect (M10), spectators (M11), economy and the 16.2 roster (M2), belts (M3), terrain (M4), fog (M5), utilities (M6), combat and performance and the GCP VM (M7), bots (M12).
**Why two slots:** a second client must see the first client's unit move, and spectators cannot claim slots (spec 6.8, 6.11).
**Why the map places the unit:** claiming a slot must create zero entities (D27, D28). Test T6 checks it.
**CEO item:** the M1 acceptance row also names a secondary executable; D02 (native versus wrapper) is open. Studio proposal: accept the browser part of M1 now, block the executable part on D02.
